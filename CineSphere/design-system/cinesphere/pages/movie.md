# Movie page overrides

Overrides `../MASTER.md` only where stated.

- **Layout:** 12-column grid; sticky poster column; showtimes and related films in the main column.
- **Images (skill):** use appropriately sized, modern-format images (`srcset`/`sizes`; TMDB serves WebP-capable sizes); reserve space to avoid layout shift.
- **Navigation (skill):** highlight the active nav item (Discover) and always offer a clear way back.
- **Trailer / booking anti-patterns:** see Master section 8.
